Stacking

Common combinations, why people pair them and how they're usually run. Use the single-compound doses in the tables above for each part. Add one compound at a time so you can tell which one causes a side effect.

Trial combination tested in human trials · Reported combination used in practice, never tested together

Recovery and repair

Injury and soft-tissue healing

BPC-157 + TB-500

Why pair them
BPC-157 is used for local repair near the injury. TB-500 acts body-wide on cell movement and new blood vessels. People pair them to cover both.
How it's run
BPC-157 250 to 500 mcg daily plus TB-500 2 to 2.5 mg twice weekly, for 4 to 6 weeks. The premixed Wolverine vial does both in one shot.
Evidence
Reported Each one has animal data only. Never studied together.

Skin, hair and repair

Glow: BPC-157 + TB-500 + GHK-Cu

Why pair them
Adds GHK-Cu, the copper peptide used for collagen and skin quality, to the repair pair.
How it's run
One premixed vial daily, dosed by the BPC-157 share, for 4 to 8 weeks.
Evidence
Reported GHK-Cu has topical skin data. The blend has none.

Repair plus gut and skin inflammation

KLOW: Glow + KPV

Why pair them
KPV is added for its anti-inflammatory effect, mostly aimed at the gut and skin.
How it's run
One premixed vial daily, dosed by the BPC-157 share.
Evidence
Reported Animal and lab data on the parts only.

Growth hormone

Sleep, recovery, body composition

CJC-1295 (no DAC) + Ipamorelin

Why pair them
CJC-1295 works through the GHRH receptor and ipamorelin through the ghrelin receptor. Hitting both at once gives a bigger growth hormone pulse than either alone.
How it's run
About 100 mcg of each in one syringe at bedtime on an empty stomach, 5 days on and 2 off, for 8 to 12 weeks.
Evidence
Reported The two-receptor effect on GH release is shown in human studies of similar compounds. The stack itself isn't trialed.

Belly fat

Tesamorelin + Ipamorelin

Why pair them
Tesamorelin has trial data for cutting visceral fat. Ipamorelin is added to strengthen the GH pulse.
How it's run
Daily, dosed by the tesamorelin share (1 to 2 mg). The 13 + 3 mg vial is premixed.
Evidence
Reported Tesamorelin alone is label-backed. The pairing isn't trialed.

Weight loss

More weight loss, better fullness

GLP-1 + Cagrilintide

Why pair them
Cagrilintide is an amylin analogue. Amylin adds a separate fullness signal on top of the GLP-1 effect.
How it's run
Both once weekly, stepped up together over several weeks. The trial pairing was semaglutide 2.4 mg with cagrilintide 2.4 mg.
Evidence
Trial for semaglutide + cagrilintide (CagriSema). Pairing it with tirzepatide or retatrutide hasn't been trialed.

Energy and metabolism

MOTS-c + NAD+

Why pair them
Both are aimed at mitochondrial function. People add them alongside a GLP-1 to offset fatigue.
How it's run
MOTS-c 5 to 10 mg a week in 2 to 3 doses, plus NAD+ 50 to 100 mg 2 to 3 times a week.
Evidence
Reported Very little human data for either.

Focus, mood and sleep

Calm focus

Selank + Semax

Why pair them
Semax is used for focus and Selank for calm. Together they're meant to sharpen focus without the edge.
How it's run
Intranasal, 250 to 500 mcg total, once or twice daily, 10 to 14 days on.
Evidence
Reported Each is registered in Russia. The pairing isn't studied.

Deeper sleep

DSIP + Ipamorelin

Why pair them
DSIP is used to help fall asleep. Ipamorelin at bedtime boosts the GH pulse that happens in deep sleep.
How it's run
DSIP 100 to 300 mcg and ipamorelin 100 to 200 mcg, about 30 minutes before bed.
Evidence
Reported DSIP's human sleep studies were mixed.

Combinations to avoid

Two GLP-1 drugs together

Tirzepatide with retatrutide, or either with semaglutide. They hit the same receptors, so side effects stack without a known benefit.

Melanotan 2 + PT-141

Both hit melanocortin receptors. Nausea, flushing and blood pressure spikes add up.

MK-677 on top of other GH peptides

Piles on water retention, hunger and higher blood sugar. Check fasting glucose if you do it.

Tesofensine + stimulants

Tesofensine already raises heart rate and blood pressure. Adding caffeine pills, pre-workout or ADHD stimulants pushes both higher.

Reconstitution instructions

How to turn a vial of freeze-dried powder into an injectable solution.

What you need
  • The peptide vial (powder), at room temperature
  • Bacteriostatic water (0.9% benzyl alcohol)
  • A 1 mL or 3 mL syringe with a needle to draw and add water
  • U-100 insulin syringes (0.3, 0.5 or 1 mL) for each dose
  • Alcohol swabs and a sharps container
1

Wash and set up

Wash your hands. Lay everything out on a clean surface. Check the label and the expiry.

2

Swab both tops

Flip off the caps. Wipe the rubber stoppers of the peptide vial and the water vial with alcohol and let them dry.

3

Draw the water

Pull back air equal to the water you need, push it into the water vial, then draw the water out. Use the calculator to pick the amount.

4

Add it down the side

Push the needle into the peptide vial at an angle and let the water run slowly down the glass wall. Never squirt it straight onto the powder.

5

Swirl, don't shake

Roll the vial gently between your palms until the powder is gone. Shaking can break the peptide apart. If it's slow to dissolve, let it sit a few minutes.

6

Check, label, refrigerate

The liquid should be clear. Write the date and the concentration on the vial and keep it at 2 to 8 °C.

7

Draw a dose

Swab the top. With an insulin syringe, draw to the unit mark the calculator gives you. Tap out air bubbles.

8

Inject and dispose

Pinch a fold of belly or thigh skin, inject at 45 to 90 degrees, and rotate sites. Put the syringe straight in the sharps container.

A cloudy solution, particles, or a color change (other than GHK-Cu's normal blue) means don't use it.

Storage

Unopened powder

Freezer at about −20 °C for the long term, or fridge at 2 to 8 °C for weeks. Keep dry and out of light.

After mixing

Fridge at 2 to 8 °C. Most mixed peptides are used within about 3 to 4 weeks. Never freeze a mixed vial.

Bacteriostatic water

Room temperature. Discard 28 days after first puncture, per the label.
